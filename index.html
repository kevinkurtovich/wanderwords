Meet the Crew

Every story is a new creation aboard the Story Ship. The same six characters, different adventures — so kids build real bonds with the crew as they build their Mandarin.

Líng 灵

The one everyone trusts. When the ship lands somewhere new and things go sideways, Líng figures out how everyone's feeling before anyone says a word. Her gold bell means everything's going to be okay.

Mèng 梦

He's staring at the clouds while everyone is panicking. He's also usually right. The smallest member of the crew, with the biggest imagination — and no interest in explaining himself.

Yǒng 勇

Every problem is an engineering challenge. He likes to build things, measure things and climbs up everywhere - with complete confidence and occasional miscalculation.

Qí 奇

He's already sprinted toward the strange door before anyone agreed it was a good idea. Qí's curiosity is what starts every adventure - and his green scarf is always windswept.

Míng 明

He's read every book on the ship. He knows what the ancient inscription says, what the legend means - and quietly, which new Mandarin words your child will learn in today's story.

Fēng 风

Fast, funny and her blue backpack always contains exactly the wrong things for the situation. Nobody knows what's in there. She zooms around with confidence and somehow manages to turn every mixup into a fun learning adventure.

The daily loop

Ten minutes. Real progress. Every day.

The WanderWords loop is designed around how children actually learn: comprehensible input, recognition, writing practice, and genuine emotional engagement.

🧠 Comprehensible Input 🔁 Spaced Repetition ✏️ Stroke-by-stroke writing 💬 Contextual meaning
01
Read today's story

Every story is written fresh — calibrated exactly to what your child already knows, with 3–5 new characters introduced naturally through the plot. No flashcards. No drills. Just a story they want to finish.

02
Review the new characters

After the story, your child reviews the characters they just met — with meaning from context they already understand. Familiar faces in familiar situations make recall natural.

03
Trace the strokes

Every character has a correct stroke order — and the app walks your child through it with real animated guidance. Writing deepens memory, connects visual and motor learning, and builds the confidence that only comes from actually doing it.

04
Level up, unlock new worlds

XP earned from reading and writing moves your child along the map — unlocking new biomes, new music, and new stories. Progress is visible, tangible, and genuinely earned.
